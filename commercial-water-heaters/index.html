How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Most tanks announce trouble weeks or months ahead with rumbling noises, rusty water, or hot showers that keep getting shorter. Keep an eye out for the following:

  • Long waits for hot water at sinks far from the heater
  • Rising gas or electric bills with no change in business volume
  • Rumbling, popping, or banging from a heavily used tank
  • Visible corrosion or water pooling near the tank
  • Water temperatures that worry you before a health inspection
  • A heater old enough that a failure feels like a matter of time
  • The tank giving up in the middle of a rush
  • Scalding bursts or wild temperature swings at showers or sinks

Why Hire a Professional for Commercial Water Heaters?

A commercial heater is not just a large residential one. Higher gas input means the supply line, meter, combustion air, and vent all have to be sized for the load. Heavy electric units can strain a panel that was never meant to carry them. Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

A few preventive steps can help you avoid a cold shower or a wet floor down the road. Put these technician-approved steps to work at home:

  • Have mixing valves checked so fixtures stay neither scalding nor lukewarm
  • Keep recirculation pumps and check valves in service to avoid long waits and wasted energy
  • Plan replacement before an aging unit fails during peak season
  • Do not store supplies against the equipment or block air openings
  • Log water temperatures at key fixtures so drift is caught early
  • Inspect anode rods regularly, since heavy use consumes them faster
  • Set a flushing schedule based on usage; busy tanks collect sediment fast

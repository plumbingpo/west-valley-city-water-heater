How South Jordan Homeowners Can Extend Water Heater Life

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year if their water is hard.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs such as discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble can often be fixed with a flush, yet that same buildup, ignored for a few years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and shorten the life of the tank, forcing a replacement much sooner. Save our number in your contacts today so help is one call away as soon as something sounds or looks wrong. Early intervention saves time, money, and ruined floors.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

In South Jordan, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tanks usually give trouble through thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor problems, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. Our technicians work on all four every week and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.

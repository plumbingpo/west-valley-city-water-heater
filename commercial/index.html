Restaurant Services

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. When the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

West Valley City Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across West Valley City and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.
